A Place With a Story

A first-class oceanfront setting with a story all its own.

Today, the Nahant Life Saving Station is a distinctive private event venue where historic New England character meets an open Atlantic horizon. The building gives your gathering a sense of place without dictating what your event has to look like.

The original boat room, broad doors, warm wood interior and oceanfront grounds create a setting that can be dressed up, kept relaxed, or transformed completely around your own style.

Built in 1898 for the U.S. Life-Saving Service and carefully restored in 2010, the Station brings authentic history to the experience — but the day belongs to you, your guests and the event you create here.

How It Works

Three steps to your date.

A simple, transparent process that confirms your date, your scheduled access time, and everything needed for a smooth event day.

— Step One

Submit Your Inquiry

Tell us your preferred date, guest count, and the kind of event you're imagining. We'll respond within two business days to confirm availability.

— Step Two

Review & Sign Contract

We'll send the rental contract and walk you through the details — partners, setup, hours, and any tenting or vendor needs.

— Step Three

Secure the Date

Your date is secured only when we receive the signed contract and full payment, including the $250 security deposit.

Scheduled Access Is Part of Your Rental Every event must include one full hour for setup and one full hour for cleanup — two hours total — within the scheduled time included in your rental fee. There is no access to the property or building before your booked start time or after your booked end time.
