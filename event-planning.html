Event Planning with Lynda

More than a beautiful setting. A beautifully planned day.

Your event should feel like you — effortless to your guests, thoughtful in every detail, and even better than you imagined. Lynda can help turn the Station's blank canvas into a celebration that brings your vision to life.

Experience That Scales

From intimate gatherings to multi-day occasions.

Lynda has planned and hosted weddings and other special occasions for groups ranging from just 10 people to more than 1,000 guests, including events extending across multiple days.

That experience brings perspective to the details that make an event work: the flow of the day, timing, vendors, setup, guest experience, transitions, contingency planning and the small touches guests remember long after the event ends.

At the Nahant Life Saving Station, that experience is paired with an unusually flexible oceanfront setting. The goal is not to impose a package or a theme. It is to help make your special day everything you wish for — and more.

Planning Support

Bring the vision. We'll help connect the details.

Planning support can help you use the Station, grounds, vendors and scheduled event time intentionally so the day feels cohesive from setup through cleanup.

— 01

Vision & event flow

Translate the feeling you want into a practical plan for the room, grounds, guest arrival, dining, celebration and transitions throughout the event.

— 02

Vendor coordination

Help align catering, bartending, rentals, tenting and other event partners so everyone understands the schedule, access and expectations.

— 03

Setup & timing

Build the required setup and cleanup periods into the event plan. Every rental includes one scheduled hour for setup and one scheduled hour for cleanup, with no access outside the booked hours.

— 04

Guest experience

Think through the moments guests actually experience — arrival, seating, food, photographs, speeches, weather alternatives and the natural rhythm of an oceanfront event.
